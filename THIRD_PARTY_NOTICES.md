## Darstellung / Rendering

MapLibre GL JS: globe and map. vis-network / vis-timeline: relationships and time. Three.js: model viewer. d3-geo: SVG globe fallback when WebGL2 is unavailable. Runtime dependencies are bundled locally.

## Fonts und Grundkarte / Fonts and basemap

Liberation Fonts: SIL Open Font License 1.1; see liberation.txt. Natural Earth 5.1.2, 1:110 million land: public domain; https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/. Source and checksum: basemap/provenance.json. Modern physical orientation, not historical borders.
